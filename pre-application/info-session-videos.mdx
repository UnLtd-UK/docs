These video recordings cover key topics from UnLtd's pre-application workshops, helping you prepare for your award application.

## Impact

This session covers how UnLtd thinks about social impact — what it means, why it matters, and how you can start articulating the difference your venture makes.

## Inclusion

This session explores equality, diversity and inclusion in the context of social entrepreneurship, and how to ensure your venture reaches and serves diverse communities.

## Sustainability

This session covers building a sustainable social venture — from financial sustainability to environmental considerations and long-term planning for impact.

## What is a Social Enterprise?

This session introduces what a social enterprise is, the different forms it can take, and how social entrepreneurs use business approaches to create positive social change.

## Application Practice Session

This session provides practical guidance on completing your UnLtd award application, with tips on what assessors look for and how to present your venture effectively.
