This series of short video guides covers the financial aspects of preparing your UnLtd award application, from understanding the basics to building your financial forecast.

<Note>
  These recordings are available as video content. Contact your UnLtd support manager for access.
</Note>

## Video 1: Introduction

An introduction to the financial guide series, explaining what will be covered and how to use these resources to prepare the financial section of your application.

## Video 2: Overview

A broad overview of the financial requirements for your UnLtd award application, including what assessors are looking for and how to present your finances clearly.

## Video 3: Historical Data

How to gather and present your historical financial data, including past income, expenditure and any existing financial records that support your application.

## Video 4: Financial Forecast

A step-by-step guide to creating a financial forecast for your social venture, covering income projections, cost estimates and cash flow planning.

## Video 5: Assumptions and Tips

How to document the assumptions behind your financial forecast, and practical tips for making your financial plan realistic, credible and compelling.
