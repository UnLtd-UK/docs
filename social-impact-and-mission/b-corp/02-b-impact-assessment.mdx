## Overview of the B Impact Assessment

The B Impact Assessment (BIA) is a free, comprehensive, and industry-specific tool designed to measure the social and environmental impacts of your company. Taking the BIA highlights the strengths of your company and shows you where you could improve across five impact areas of your business. To progress with B Corp Certification, your company must reach a total verified score of at least **80 points out of 200** available on the BIA.

The assessment balances two dimensions of your impact:

- **Operational Impact** -- Measures the day-to-day impacts of running your business, such as the environmental footprint of your facilities, your interaction with your local community, your workplace practices, your purchases, and your governance structure. This section is independent of business design or intent. The BIA aligns with other best-in-class standards and rolls in other certifications (such as LEED), so you earn points for certifications your company has already achieved.

- **Impact Business Models** -- Measures whether the intentional design of your company creates specific, positive outcomes for one or more of your stakeholders. This may be a product, beneficiary, business process, or activity, such as annually donating five percent of revenue, being worker-owned, or serving an underserved market. No other certification provides this review.

**Understanding the difference:** If your office recycles, that is an Operational Impact. If your business model is to recycle electronics, that is an Impact Business Model.

## The Five Impact Areas

The BIA evaluates your company across five pillars:

### Governance

Evaluates your company's overall mission, ethics, accountability, and transparency. This area measures whether you have adopted a social or environmental mission and how you engage employees, board members, and the community to achieve it. It also assesses employee access to financial information, customer feedback opportunities, and the diversity of your governing bodies.

### Workers

Assesses your company's relationship with its workforce, including compensation, benefits, training, and ownership opportunities. This area also focuses on the overall work environment, covering management and worker communication, job flexibility, corporate culture, and worker health and safety practices.

### Community

Evaluates your supplier relations, diversity, and involvement in the local community. It also measures your practices and policies around community service and charitable giving, including whether your product or service is designed to solve a social issue such as access to basic services, health, education, economic opportunity, and the arts.

### Environment

Evaluates your environmental performance through facilities, materials, emissions, and resource and energy use. You will answer questions about transportation and distribution channels, as well as the environmental impact of your supply chain. This area also measures whether your products or services are designed to solve environmental issues, such as providing renewable energy, conserving resources, reducing waste, or preventing pollution.

### Customers

Measures the impact your company has on its customers by focusing on whether you sell products or services that promote public benefit and whether those offerings target underserved populations. This area also covers whether your product or service is designed to solve a social or environmental issue.

## Assessment Tools and Features

Each question on the BIA includes helpful tools:

| Tool | What It Does |
| --- | --- |
| **Explain This** | Provides definitions and rationale for why the practice is deemed important, plus specific instructions to help you answer accurately |
| **Show Example** | Provides examples of businesses already practicing the topic |
| **In Practice** | Links to best-practice guides and implementation tools |
| **Star** | Adds a question to your Improvement Report |
| **Bookmark** | Adds a question to your Revisit Question Report |

### Improvement Report

This customised report allows you to navigate the assessment by jumping through Section, Impact Area, Difficulty of Change, and Score Value. The report contains all operational questions where there is an opportunity for improvement because your company has not earned full credit. You can identify questions to target for future improvement by clicking the star icon on any question.

### Revisit Question Report

This report shows you all the questions you marked to revisit at a later time. All skipped questions are automatically listed here too. You can answer questions within the report without going through the full assessment again.

## Taking Your First Pass

Start by taking a first pass through the assessment, estimating some of your answers so you can get an initial score. Some questions may not be relevant to your business -- select "N/A" for those. The BIA uses a positive points system, so there is a large volume of questions designed to uncover all the ways your business may be making an impact.

**Tips for your first pass:**

- Be conservative with your answers
- Use the "Revisit" function (bookmark icon) to flag questions you want to return to later
- Use the revision, improvement, and B Impact report icons to access useful benchmark data
- Identify areas that require gathering additional information
- See how your company compares globally with other companies

Note that policy questions typically carry a lower weighting (around 0.25 to 0.5 points each), while some operational and impact model questions carry significantly more weight. Consider creating a tracking spreadsheet to monitor the questions you have answered, identify areas for improvement, and note the score differential available in each area.

### Registration

To ensure the assessment questions are relevant, the first step is to input information about the location, sector, industry, and size of your company. These registration responses determine the questions available on your assessment track. If you need to adjust your registration, contact [support@bcorporation.net](mailto:support@bcorporation.net).

### Disclosure Questionnaire

After completing 90 percent of the BIA, you should begin the Disclosure Questionnaire. This is confidential and has no effect on your overall score. It is a place where you can share any information not captured by the BIA. It may require sensitive information and input from your legal team or company executives. Your Disclosure Questionnaire does not need to be finished for B Lab to begin reviewing your assessment, but it needs to be in progress.
