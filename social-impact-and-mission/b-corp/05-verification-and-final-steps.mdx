## Reaching the Verification Stage

If your company achieves a score of 80 or higher on the B Impact Assessment, congratulations -- you have become eligible to certify as a B Corporation. There are four important steps to complete before you officially certify.

## Step 1: Submit Your Assessment

Once you complete at least 90 percent of the assessment, you need to:

1. Complete a **Company Profile** (available within the BIA) with additional information relevant to the review of your assessment
2. Begin the **Disclosure Questionnaire** (also available within the BIA), which requires sensitive information and may need input from your legal team or company executives
3. Submit your assessment for review

## Step 2: Pre-Review Call and Verification Report

After you submit, you will receive an email from B Lab within a couple of days confirming receipt and asking you to schedule a review call with a Standards Analyst. The system randomly selects six to 15 questions from where you are scoring points on the assessment. You will be asked to upload supporting documents for those questions to the Verification Report.

**Key timelines:**

- Upload required documents at least **two business days** before your review call
- You typically have **two weeks** between initial submission and your scheduled review call

The list of required documents becomes available only after your review call has been scheduled. If you do not have the necessary documents, you can download templates provided in the Verification Report.

### Inside the Verification Report

| Feature | What It Does |
| --- | --- |
| **Document Instructions** | Tells you the specific document requirement for each question |
| **Download Template** | Provides a template if you do not have the required document |
| **Attach Document or Share URL** | Lets you upload documents or share a link (e.g., Google Drive, Box). Make sure B Lab has permission to access shared URLs |
| **Leave Comments** | Allows you to communicate additional information about your uploaded documents |

### Tips for Submitting Documentation

- Make sure you fully understand the question -- read the "Explain This," "In Practice," and "Show Example" tabs
- Highlight relevant areas of the document (for example, if you upload your employee handbook to verify a parental leave policy, highlight those paragraphs)
- If the question is multiple choice, make sure each selected item has supporting documentation
- When possible, upload Excel spreadsheets rather than PDFs so the Standards Analyst can easily verify percentages

## Step 3: Review Call

Your review call is a **60 to 90 minute** call during which a Standards Analyst will review both your assessment answers and the documentation you provided. The purpose is to ensure that questions are answered as intended and that your company's impact is fully and accurately captured.

The Standards Analyst will help clarify how questions apply to your business and will make edits to the assessment. All companies can expect to see a score change during the review process. On average, companies have seen a **10-point change** in score (both positive and negative).

### Review Call Prep Checklist

- Upload your supporting documents at least two days beforehand
- Have the assessment and supporting documents open in front of you
- Write down a list of questions for your Standards Analyst -- these can be about the BIA, the process, and the requirements for certification

## Step 4: Post-Review Call Documentation

After the review call, you will need to:

1. Upload documentation about questions pertaining to your company's **Impact Business Model** (following the same process as pre-call documentation)
2. Upload any requested **follow-up documentation** to the questions discussed on the call

After the review call, do not make changes to the assessment answers yourself. If you see something that is incorrect, contact your Standards Analyst with the question details, old answer, proposed new answer, and supporting documentation.

## Signing the B Corp Agreement

Once you have met the verified 80-point threshold, met (or committed to meeting) the legal requirement, and submitted your supporting documentation, you are ready to sign the B Corp Agreement.

Your certification term is **three years**. After three years, you must complete an updated B Impact Assessment and go through the verification process again to maintain certification. Your agreement also commits you to participate in a site review if your company is one of the 10 percent of B Corps selected that year. The site review may be conducted in person or virtually.

You will also sign the **Declaration of Interdependence**, which is included in the B Corp Agreement and outlines the values that define the B Corp community.

## Certification Fees

Your company pays an annual certification fee based on a tiered system determined by annual revenue.

| Company Revenue Range | Annual Fee |
| --- | --- |
| $0 -- $149,999 | $500 |
| $150,000 -- $1,999,999 | $1,000 |
| $2,000,000 -- $4,999,999 | $1,500 |
| $5,000,000 -- $9,999,999 | $2,500 |
| $10,000,000 -- $19,999,999 | $5,000 |
| $20,000,000 -- $49,999,999 | $10,000 |
| $50,000,000 -- $74,999,999 | $15,000 |
| $75,000,000 -- $99,999,999 | $20,000 |
| $100M+ | Varied |

## Public Transparency

Once you complete your certification, a public profile with your company's score and your impact report will go live on [bcorporation.net](https://www.bcorporation.net). For certain companies, additional transparency requirements as outlined in the B Corp Agreement may apply.
