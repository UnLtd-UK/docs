## What Is a B Corp?

Certified B Corporations, or B Corps, are for-profit companies at the heart of a global movement of people using the power of business to create shared, durable prosperity for all. More than 2,500 B Corps operate in over 150 industries across 60 countries worldwide. Your business can play an important role in growing this movement and changing the way business is done for good.

Becoming a B Corp is not just about achieving a certification or seal of approval. It is about joining a community of businesses dedicated to the same vision and goal. B Corps are committed to improving their practices and increasing their impact. The path to certification illuminates areas of positive impact and surfaces aspects where more work is needed.

## Why B Corp Certification Matters

Consumer research consistently shows that people care about the businesses behind the products they buy:

- **90%** of Americans say they care that a business is beneficial, not just the product or service (Cone Communications)
- **73%** of conscious consumers care about the company, not just the product (BBMG)
- **Less than 1%** of conscious consumers trust company ads or packaging statements when assessing whether a company does what it says (BBMG)
- **32%** of consumers will pay more for products or services from companies committed to positive social or environmental impact (Nielsen, 2014)

These numbers make it clear: third-party verification of your company's impact carries far more weight than self-reported claims.

## Benefits of Certification

### Join a Community

The B Corp community sets a gold standard for businesses that are not only best in the world, but best for the world. By joining a global community of high-performing, mission-driven businesses, you can collaborate with fellow B Corp leaders and employees, share best practices, and build a collective voice. The community connects through face-to-face events, a peer-to-peer online network called the B Hive, and other B Corp collaborations.

> "One of the biggest benefits is collaboration. The certification gives us an opportunity to get to know B Lab and other B Corps, learn from them, and share with them our best practices. The certification also keeps us alert to improvement opportunities within the company and to the changing sustainable business climate outside the company."
> -- Kenyatta Brame, Chief Administration Officer at Cascade Engineering

### Attract Talent and Build Culture

Millennials and younger generations increasingly seek to work for companies with a stated mission and want to bring their whole selves to work. B Corps report that after certification, the quality of hires, retention rate, and employee commitment and excitement all increased.

> "[B Corp certification] allowed us to be a business set apart from businesses... this would allow us to really dive into recruitment and retention in a really special way where we all know the mission and we're dedicated to move toward it together."
> -- Colette Kessler, Business Development Manager at Change.org

### Benchmark and Improve Performance

Completing the B Impact Assessment is often the first time a business measures and benchmarks key aspects of its performance. After the assessment, you have an efficient way to compare with other companies, improve your practices, and quantify your impact. You can use tools like the "Set a Goal" and "Mark to Revisit" features to track improvements over time.

> "B Corp certification has given us a way to measure things that aren't inherently quantitative, but we know are important to us... It helps us to bring that into a measurement space where we can compare ourselves against other companies and see are we really being leaders in this area or is there a way we can improve?"
> -- Katie Wallace, Assistant Director of Sustainability, New Belgium Brewing Company

### Build Credibility and Amplify Your Voice

Having a third-party, whole-business certification helps your company stand out in the marketplace. It serves as a shorthand for great practices you may or may not communicate to your customers. Certifying as a B Corp also gives your company an edge in gaining press coverage, and you will have the option to tell your stories on B the Change, the online publication dedicated to the B Corp movement.

### Protect Your Mission

The B Corp legal framework -- the benefit corporation governance structure -- helps you protect your company's mission through capital raises and leadership changes. This governance structure gives you more flexibility when evaluating potential sale and liquidity options, ensuring your values are baked into your company's legal DNA.

## The Steps to Certification

Your journey to B Corp certification follows five main steps:

1. **Articulate the business case** -- Understand why certification matters for your specific business
2. **Engage your leadership and board** -- Get buy-in from key decision-makers
3. **Complete the B Impact Assessment** -- Take the rigorous assessment that measures your impact
4. **Meet the legal requirement** -- Align your legal structure with your mission
5. **Verification** -- Submit documentation and complete a review call with B Lab
