## Why the Legal Requirement Matters

B Corp certification requires all companies to align their legal structures with the mission of their company. By adopting B Corp legal standards, your company will:

- Bake the values of your company into its legal DNA
- Obtain legal protection and permission to consider nonfinancial stakeholders in decision-making
- Ensure that your mission is protected through time, including through changes in management or ownership

**Do not leave the legal requirement until the end of your certification process.** Legal requirements vary by jurisdiction, so investigate your options early and create a timeline with your leadership.

## Legal Requirement Timelines

Your company's legal requirement, and the timeline for the process, will be determined by your corporate structure and your country and state of incorporation.

### If Benefit Corporation or SPC-Equivalent Status Is Available

Your company must become a benefit corporation or adopt Social Purpose Corporation (SPC)-equivalent status within **two years** after your initial certification date. If your state passes benefit corporation or SPC-equivalent legislation after your company has certified, you will have until the end of your subsequent two-year certification term (following the passage of the legislation) to meet the requirement.

### If Benefit Corporation Status Is Unavailable but Constituency Status Exists

- **Corporations** have until **one year** after certification
- **LLCs** have until **90 days** after certification

To complete the process:

1. Get your amended articles approved by both your board of directors and shareholders (requiring either a majority or supermajority vote, depending on your state)
2. File your amended articles with the secretary of state, coupled with a modest fee (usually less than $300)

### If You Are a Sole Proprietor

As a sole proprietor, your organisation is not formally organised as a corporation and has no corporate governing documents. There is no additional legal requirement to attain certification.

## Where to Find Legal Resources

- Visit [benefitcorp.net](https://www.benefitcorp.net) to access attorneys, state-by-state statutes, and other resources to help your company through the legal process
- Visit [bcorporation.net/certification/legal-requirements](https://bcorporation.net/certification/legal-requirements) to use the legal tool filter, which identifies your company's legal requirements based on your location of incorporation and corporate structure

Meeting the legal requirement in advance can earn you between **5 and 7.5 points** on the B Impact Assessment. If none of the specified legal options are available to you, you can still earn **2.5 points** by signing the B Corp Agreement.
