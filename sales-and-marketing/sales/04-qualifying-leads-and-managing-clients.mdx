## Why Qualification Matters

Not every lead is worth pursuing, and not every client deserves the same level of investment. Qualification is the discipline of assessing opportunities systematically so you spend your time where it will have the greatest return.

Good qualification helps you:

- Focus your limited sales resources on the most promising prospects.
- Avoid wasting time on deals that are unlikely to close.
- Identify which existing client relationships to invest in and which to renegotiate.
- Build a more accurate sales forecast.

## The BANT Framework

BANT is a widely used qualification framework that evaluates prospects across four criteria:

| Letter | Criterion | Key question |
|---|---|---|
| **B** | Budget | Does the prospect have the budget for your product or service? |
| **A** | Authority | Are you speaking to the person who can authorise the purchase? |
| **N** | Need | Does the prospect genuinely need what you offer? |
| **T** | Timeline | Will this deal happen within a reasonable timeframe (typically the next three months)? |

A prospect that scores well across all four criteria is a high-priority lead. One that fails on multiple criteria may not be worth pursuing right now.

### Beyond BANT: Additional Qualifying Questions

Depending on your context, you may want to add bespoke qualifying questions. Consider:

- Is the money worth it relative to the effort required?
- How closely does this opportunity align with your mission and values?
- What is the opportunity cost of pursuing this deal?
- Do you have the right skills and contacts to deliver?
- What could this lead to in the future -- repeat business, referrals, or a case study?

## Lead Qualification Scoring

### BANTS Sales Qualifier

The BANTS Sales Qualifier is a spreadsheet tool that scores each lead against six qualifying questions -- the four core BANT criteria plus two customisable questions you define for your own context.

**How it works:**

| Column | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Client Name** | The name of the prospect or organisation |
| **Deal Size** | Estimated monetary value of the opportunity |
| **Qualifying Questions** | Six questions per lead based on BANT plus your bespoke criteria |
| **Yes / No** | Your answer to each question |
| **Score** | 1 for Yes, 0 for No (calculated automatically) |
| **Likelihood (out of 6)** | Total score across all six questions |
| **Notes** | Free-text field for additional context |

**To use it:**

1. Enter each prospective client and estimated deal size.
2. Answer each of the six qualifying questions with Yes or No.
3. The score column calculates automatically.
4. The likelihood column totals your scores out of 6.
5. Use the likelihood rating to prioritise which leads to pursue first.
6. Customise the two bespoke questions to suit your specific sales context.

### Worked Example: Health-Tech Qualifier

The MyCognition Sales Qualifier demonstrates how to adapt the BANTS template for a specific product and market. In this health-tech example, the six qualifying questions have been tailored:

1. Do they have the budget?
2. Are you speaking to the budget holder?
3. Do they have a clinical or social need for our product?
4. Is the client engaged with us (e.g., responsive to emails)?
5. Will this deal happen within the next three months?
6. Does the client have a clear rationale to buy (e.g., ROI)?

**Example scores from the template:**

| Prospect | Deal Size | Score (out of 6) |
|---|---|---|
| Telford Project | 35,000 | 3 |
| Academies S Korea | 15,000 | 3 |
| IQVIA | 20,000 | 1 |
| Guardian Caregivers | 32,500 | 0 (not yet assessed) |

This example shows how the same framework produces very different priority levels for different prospects, helping you decide where to focus first.

### Lead Likelihood Scorecard

For a deeper assessment of new opportunities, the Lead Qualification Scorecard evaluates each lead against 12 questions grouped into three categories. Each question is scored as No (0 points), Maybe (1 point), or Yes (2 points), producing a normalised **Sale Likelihood Score**.

#### Are They Serious?

| Question |
|---|
| Will there be consequences of inaction? |
| Do they have the authority to make this decision? |
| Do they have the resources to manage the platform? |
| Do they believe there is a strong economic case for change? |

#### Are You Likely to Win?

| Question |
|---|
| Do their needs align with your most powerful capability? |
| Have you been able to influence the right person? |
| Do you understand their decision-making criteria? |
| Do you have a major advantage over your competitors? |

#### Is It Really Worth the Effort?

| Question |
|---|
| Will it take a small amount of effort to win this opportunity? |
| Does their attitude to partnership match yours? |
| Does their perspective on cost and value match yours? |
| Could this evolve into a long-standing relationship? |

**To use it:**

1. Enter the organisation name.
2. Score each of the 12 questions (No, Maybe, or Yes).
3. Review the total score and sale likelihood percentage.
4. Compare scores across multiple leads to decide where to focus.

## Client Value Assessment

Once you have won a client, qualification does not stop. The Client Value Scorecard helps you evaluate whether existing clients are worth continued investment. It scores each client across 12 questions in three categories, using the same No (0), Yes and No (1), or Yes (2) scoring system.

### Are They Profitable?

| Question |
|---|
| Do you charge accurately on a time-and-materials basis? |
| Are they on your latest service or platform rates? |
| Is this client proactive, engaged, and responsive? |
| Are you doing the most you can to maximise profits? |

### Are They Strategically Important?

| Question |
|---|
| Do their needs align with your most powerful capability? |
| Have you been able to influence the right person? |
| Are you likely to win more work from them, or via association with them? |
| Are they an important client in your portfolio? |

### Are They Worth the Effort?

| Question |
|---|
| Do you enjoy working with this client? |
| Does their attitude to partnership match yours? |
| Does their perspective on cost and value match yours? |
| Could this evolve into a long-standing relationship? |

The scorecard calculates a normalised **Client Score** (higher is better). Use this to compare clients side by side and identify which relationships to invest in, which to renegotiate, and which to deprioritise.

**To use it:**

1. Enter the organisation name.
2. Score each of the 12 questions.
3. Review the total score and client score percentage.
4. Compare scores across your client portfolio to guide relationship investment decisions.

## A Note on CRM

Effective qualification and client management depend on having a system to track your interactions and relationships. A CRM (Customer Relationship Management) approach shifts your perspective from purely acquiring new customers to retaining and growing existing ones. This builds repeat business, generates referrals, and reduces your cost of sale over time. For more on building a customer retention strategy, see the resources in the CRM section of this toolkit.
