## Getting Started with Amazon

Creating an Amazon seller account allows you to list and sell your items directly on Amazon's UK website, with Amazon handling payment and, if required, fulfilment. You can also access several additional programmes, including Amazon Business for B2B sales, Amazon Global Selling, and specialist marketplaces such as Amazon Handmade, Amazon Renewed, and Amazon Launchpad.

## Choosing Your Seller Account

There are two types of seller account, and your choice depends on your sales volume:

| Account Type | Best For | Cost |
|-------------|----------|------|
| **Individual** | Sellers with fewer than 35 items per month | £0.75 per item sold (excluding VAT) plus referral fees (typically 7–15%) |
| **Professional** | Sellers with more than 35 items per month | £25/month (excluding VAT) plus referral fees, with access to selling tools and reports |

Many product categories can only be listed by traders with a Professional account who have been approved by Amazon. These include clothing, jewellery, music and DVDs, and organic food. For more information about categories requiring approval, visit [Amazon's category requirements](https://sellercentral.amazon.co.uk/help/hub/reference/external/200333160?locale=en-GB).

## Programmes for Small Business Traders

Once you have a seller account, you can access a range of additional programmes:

### Amazon Business

Provides features for B2B sales such as quantity-based discounts, VAT-exclusive price display, and the ability to offer business customers a Pay by Invoice option. Amazon handles credit risk assessment, billing, and payment collection. Learn more at [Amazon Business](https://sell.amazon.co.uk/programmes/amazon-business).

### Amazon Handmade

A marketplace for genuinely handmade items. Product listings are free with a 12.24% referral fee for every item sold. Apply at [Amazon Handmade](https://sell.amazon.co.uk/programmes/handmade).

### Amazon Custom

Enables you to offer products that can be customised with text, images, logos, or other options. Visit [Amazon Custom](https://sell.amazon.co.uk/programmes/custom-products).

### Amazon Launchpad

Aimed at start-ups and small businesses selling unique, innovative, or sustainable products. Benefits include strategic business support and specialised customer engagement tools. Details at [Amazon Launchpad](https://sell.amazon.co.uk/programmes/launchpad).

### Amazon Renewed

A marketplace for selling high-quality like-new products (refurbished and pre-owned) to Amazon customers worldwide. See [Amazon Renewed](https://sell.amazon.co.uk/programmes/renewed).

### Specialist Marketplaces

Amazon also offers marketplaces for selling [apps](https://developer.amazon.com/apps-and-games), [software](https://aws.amazon.com/marketplace/partners/management-tour), and [self-published books](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US).

## Order Fulfilment and Delivery

You have two main options for getting products to customers:

### Fulfilment By Amazon (FBA)

Most traders use FBA, which works as follows:

1. You send your products to Amazon's fulfilment centres
2. Amazon stores your inventory
3. When an item is purchased, Amazon picks, packs, and ships it
4. Amazon handles customer service and returns

Using FBA involves paying a fulfilment fee and a monthly storage fee, but it can make your products more visible — items sold via FBA are eligible for Amazon Prime delivery options.

### Self-Fulfilment

If you prefer to manage your own logistics — for example because you sell hazardous goods that do not meet fulfilment centre requirements, or seasonal products that would incur high storage fees — you can store and ship items directly to customers yourself.

For more information, visit [Amazon's fulfilment options](https://sell.amazon.co.uk/fulfil).

## Selling Internationally

Amazon provides several programmes to help you sell to customers overseas:

- **Amazon Global Selling** — expand into Amazon marketplaces in North America, Australia, Japan, and the Middle East ([learn more](https://sell.amazon.co.uk/global-selling))
- **Pan-European FBA** — reduce costs of selling to European customers by storing products in fulfilment centres across Germany, France, Italy, and Spain ([learn more](https://sell.amazon.co.uk/fulfilment-by-amazon/fba-europe))
- **Customs clearance and shipping support** — for traders who fulfil international orders themselves ([learn more](https://sell.amazon.co.uk/compliance-solutions/international-trade))

## Advertising on Amazon

Amazon provides several ways to promote your products:

| Ad Type | What It Does |
|---------|-------------|
| **Sponsored Products** | Places ads within search results and on product pages, targeting people browsing for similar products |
| **Amazon Stores** | Customisable web pages to showcase your brand and products using templates, videos, text, and images |
| **Sponsored Brands** | Ads in search results featuring your brand logo, customised information, and links to multiple products |
| **Sponsored Display** | Ads that appear on Amazon pages and third-party websites |

For more information, visit [Amazon Advertising](https://sell.amazon.co.uk/advertising).

## Other Ways to Earn Revenue

Beyond selling products directly, Amazon offers additional revenue opportunities:

### Amazon Associates

An affiliate marketing programme that lets you earn revenue by advertising Amazon products on your website, blog, or social media account. When a sale is generated through your link, you earn a fee. Visit [Amazon Associates](https://affiliate-program.amazon.co.uk/).

### Delivery Services

- **Amazon Flex** — deliver parcels on behalf of Amazon using your own vehicle ([learn more](https://flex.amazon.co.uk/?ref_=azuk_gen_all_flex))
- **Amazon Delivery Service Partner** — a more structured delivery business opportunity ([learn more](https://logistics.amazon.co.uk/marketing))

### Amazon Hub

If you have a physical location, you can host an Amazon Hub click-and-collect kiosk to benefit from increased foot traffic and sales.
