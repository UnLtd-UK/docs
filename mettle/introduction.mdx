**Mettle** is one of two pillars in UnLtd's support framework. It focuses on the practical foundations you need to build, run and grow a sustainable social venture. From setting up your legal structure and managing your finances, to developing your product, reaching customers and securing investment, Mettle gives you the knowledge and tools to get it right.

## How you can access support

Your Support Manager will help you identify which Mettle resources are most relevant to you right now. You can also access support through:

- **One-to-one coaching** — at least 4 sessions through your award
- **Pro-bono consultancy** — legal partners offering advice on structures, IP, leases and contracts
- **Workshops** — free sessions on financial management, marketing and more
- **Mentoring** — expert advice from our network of business professionals
