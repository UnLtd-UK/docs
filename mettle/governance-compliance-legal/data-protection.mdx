The below checklist outlines some key data protection considerations for early-stage businesses in the
UK. (Note that this checklist focuses on compliance with UK data protection laws, if you process personal data
of individuals outside of the UK, you will need to consider additional data protection laws).
The regulator for data protection in the UK is the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO). The ICO’s website
contains very useful guidance about how to comply with UK data protection laws. Please see here for specific
advice targeted at small to medium sized organisations.

Personal data includes any information which directly (i.e., name, address, email address) or indirectly (i.e., IP address,

personal data.

What happens if you do not comply with your obligations?
Note that the ICO has the power to take enforcement action (such as fines) against organisations that do not comply with
requirements under UK data protection law. The ICO has outlined some key tips for small businesses to avoid receiving a
fine. Whilst it is important to get to grips with your data protection compliance, it is worth noting that the risk of enforcement
action tends to increase with size, scale and complexity of personal data handling. As such, it tends to be the larger
corporations and organisations conducting particularly high risk and sensitive data processing that are more at risk of
regulatory action than smaller, early-stage businesses.
The above list outlines some of the initial key considerations for early-stage business, however it is not an
exhaustive list of all UK data protection law requirements. You should seek input from your legal advisor for any
more detailed analysis.
