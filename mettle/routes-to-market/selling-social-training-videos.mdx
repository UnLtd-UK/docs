These training video recordings accompany the Selling Social programme, covering key concepts and techniques for selling your social venture's products and services.

<Note>

## Think You Know Your Customers?

This session challenges assumptions about who your customers are and helps you develop a deeper understanding of their needs, motivations and behaviours.

## The Social Canvas

This session introduces the Social Selling Canvas — a framework for mapping out how your social venture creates, delivers and communicates value to customers.

## Why Should Your Customers Care?

This session explores how to articulate your unique value proposition and communicate why customers should choose your product or service over alternatives.

## Selling Social Overview

A general overview recording covering the fundamentals of selling social — how social enterprises can effectively market and sell their products and services while staying true to their mission.
