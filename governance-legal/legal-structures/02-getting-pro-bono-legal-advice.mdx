## Accessing Free Legal Support

If you are a social entrepreneur supported by UnLtd, you may be able to access pro bono (free) legal advice to help with the legal aspects of setting up or running your venture. This service connects you with volunteer legal professionals who can assist with questions about legal structures, contracts, intellectual property and other business law matters.

## What You Need to Prepare

When requesting pro bono legal advice, you will typically need to provide:

### About You and Your Venture
- Your name and contact details (telephone and email)
- Your venture's name and a brief description of what it does
- Your current legal structure (e.g. sole trader, CIC, limited company)
- Your address and registered office (if applicable)

### About Your Legal Question
- Details of the legal advice you need --- be as specific as possible
- Names of any other parties involved in the matter
- Any relevant background documents or correspondence

### Your UnLtd Details
- The name of your UnLtd Support Manager or Award Manager
- The date of your UnLtd award
- Your understanding of the issue on a scale of 1 to 5

## What the Service Can Help With

Pro bono legal advice typically covers:

- **Legal structure decisions** --- choosing or changing your business structure
- **Company formation** --- setting up articles of association and other founding documents
- **Contracts** --- reviewing or drafting agreements with suppliers, partners or customers
- **Intellectual property** --- protecting your brand, inventions or creative works
- **Employment law** --- basic questions about hiring staff or engaging volunteers
- **Data protection** --- understanding your obligations under the GDPR
- **Governance** --- questions about directors' duties, board structures and compliance

## Important Notes

- Pro bono advice is provided by volunteers and availability may vary
- The service is designed for initial guidance rather than ongoing legal representation
- For complex matters, your pro bono adviser may recommend instructing a paid solicitor
- Your data will be shared with relevant partners and volunteers to facilitate your request

For more information about UnLtd's support services and privacy policy, visit [unltd.org.uk](https://www.unltd.org.uk).
