## Accessing Export Support

Exporters throughout the UK can access a variety of government support initiatives to help enter or develop new overseas markets. This page covers the main support schemes available, including advice on trading internationally, financial support for overseas trade visits, and access to export finance and insurance.

## UK-Wide Support

### Department for Business and Trade

The Department for Business and Trade provides specialist support for new and existing exporters:

- **International Trade Advisers** --- face-to-face advice and practical support about expanding overseas. Find your local team at [great.gov.uk/contact/office-finder](https://www.great.gov.uk/contact/office-finder)
- **Export Support Service** --- submit questions and receive answers about entering new markets, paperwork and country-specific rules. Visit [gov.uk/ask-export-support-team](https://www.gov.uk/ask-export-support-team)
- **UK Export Academy** --- events and workshops to help develop your export knowledge. Visit [great.gov.uk/export-academy](https://www.great.gov.uk/export-academy/)
- **Trade specialists** --- help with preparing for trade shows, overseas market visits, and introductions to buyers, agents and distributors. Visit [gov.uk](https://www.gov.uk/overseas-customers-export-opportunities/trade-specialist-help)
- **Export opportunities database** --- more than 41,000 export sales leads across a range of sectors. Visit [great.gov.uk/export-opportunities](https://www.great.gov.uk/export-opportunities)
- **Digital Commerce Advisers** --- tailored advice on using international online marketplaces. Visit [great.gov.uk](https://www.great.gov.uk/campaigns/digital-commerce-export-support)
- **Online supplier directory** --- showcase your business to overseas buyers. Visit [great.gov.uk/find-a-buyer](https://www.great.gov.uk/find-a-buyer)
- **Overseas Business Risk guides** --- detailed information about potential risks when trading overseas. Visit [gov.uk](https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/overseas-business-risk)
- **Trade compliance tools** --- free online tools to help you understand duties, customs procedures, commodity codes and export licences. Visit [gov.uk/browse/business/exports](https://www.gov.uk/browse/business/exports)

### UK Export Finance (UKEF)

UK Export Finance is the UK Government's export credit agency. UKEF can provide:

- Credit to facilitate overseas purchases of UK exports
- Credit insurance policies covering you against non-payment by overseas customers
- Overseas investment insurance covering losses from political events in countries you trade with

UKEF schemes are commonly used by larger businesses and established exporters. Visit [gov.uk](https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/our-products) for details of all UKEF products and services.

### Music Export Growth Scheme

Grants from £5,000 to £50,000 for UK-registered independent music companies to assist with international marketing campaigns. Applicants must part-fund the project themselves and provide detailed plans. Visit [bpi.co.uk](https://www.bpi.co.uk/news-analysis/music-export-growth-scheme) for more information.

## Regional Support

### Scotland

Scottish Enterprise provides face-to-face advice and practical support including:

- Guidance on compliance requirements when trading overseas
- The International Market Research Service for overseas market research
- The Preparing to Export Programme for new exporters developing an export plan
- The International Ecommerce Programme for selling via online marketplaces
- The GlobalScot network matching new exporters with experienced mentors
- Trade missions and exhibitions

Visit [scottish-enterprise.com](https://www.scottish-enterprise.com/support-for-businesses/exports-and-international-markets) for more information.

### Wales

Business Wales provides advice, training and practical support including:

- The [Export Hub](https://export.businesswales.gov.wales/en) with information about marketplaces, customs duties, import/export controls and country-specific profiles
- Workshops, webinars, guides and case studies on export strategy
- [Trade missions and exhibitions](https://businesswales.gov.wales/export/overseas-events)

### Northern Ireland

Invest Northern Ireland provides:

- Support developing an export strategy, carrying out market research and connecting with customers. Visit [investni.com](https://www.investni.com/support-for-business/develop-new-export-markets)
- The Practical Export Skills programme with workshops on documentation, logistics and networking
- A business information service for overseas market research
- Trade missions and exhibitions

## Assessing Trade Risks

When expanding into new markets, you should assess the risks that geopolitical changes pose to your business. Consider evaluating your exposure across these dimensions:

| Risk Factor | Questions to Ask |
|---|---|
| **Income sources** | How much of your income comes directly or indirectly from markets that may be affected by trade policy changes? |
| **Income concentration** | What percentage of your total income is at risk? |
| **Contract security** | How secure are your existing contracts and relationships? |
| **Ability to pivot** | Can you redirect affected income streams to sustainable alternatives? |

### Mitigation Strategies

- Diversify your income streams across different markets and sectors
- Pivot your business model where needed
- Explore new international trade opportunities
- Protect your intellectual property through trade marks and registrations in target markets
