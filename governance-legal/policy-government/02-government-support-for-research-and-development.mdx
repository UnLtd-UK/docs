## Funding Your Innovation

The UK Government and devolved administrations have a strategy to stimulate business research and development (R&D) and encourage innovative firms to contribute to economic growth. A range of financial support is available to help your business undertake world-class innovation and R&D.

## UK-Wide Funding Programmes

### Innovate UK Funding Competitions

Innovate UK runs funding competitions providing between £25,000 and £10 million for R&D projects. You can use the funding to:

- Test and develop an innovative idea
- Create new knowledge or develop a new process, product or service
- Work with other firms or research organisations on collaborative projects

The Open Funding Programme provides funding for innovative businesses with high-growth potential. Visit [gov.uk](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/innovation-apply-for-a-funding-award) for more information.

### Knowledge Transfer Partnerships (KTPs)

KTPs help your firm improve competitiveness, productivity and performance by accessing knowledge and expertise from UK universities and colleges. A KTP is formed between your company and an academic institution, employing recently qualified Associates (typically postgraduate researchers or graduates) to work on a project of strategic importance.

- Projects run between 12 and 36 months
- Part-funded by the Department for Business, Energy and Industrial Strategy (BEIS)
- Applications can be made throughout the year

Visit [ktp.innovateuk.org](http://ktp.innovateuk.org) for more information.

### Small Business Research Initiative (SBRI)

The SBRI addresses public sector challenges by funding innovative solutions from industry. It generates opportunities for early-stage and SME businesses to work with public bodies. Successful applicants receive a contract covering the full cost of demonstrating feasibility, with the offer of further funding for prototype development, while retaining their intellectual property rights.

Visit [gov.uk](https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/sbri-the-small-business-research-initiative) for more information.

### Nesta

Innovation charity Nesta funds ideas with the potential to improve people's lives across sectors from healthcare and education to creative industries. Support includes:

- Grant funding, direct investment and challenge prizes
- The [Creative Business Mentor Network](https://www.nesta.org.uk/project/creative-business-mentor-network/) providing one-to-one mentoring for creative firms
- [Nesta Impact Investments](https://nestainvestments.org.uk) for organisations showing high potential for social impact and return on investment

Visit [nesta.org.uk/help-me-innovate](https://www.nesta.org.uk/help-me-innovate/) for more information.

## Tax Relief for R&D

### R&D Tax Relief

R&D tax relief can reduce your corporation tax bill through two main schemes:

| Scheme | Eligible Firms | Benefit |
|---|---|---|
| **SME Scheme** | Small and medium-sized enterprises | 230% relief on allowable R&D costs (an additional £130 deduction for every £100 spent), or a cash tax credit |
| **RDEC Scheme** | Larger companies (and SMEs sub-contracted by large firms) | 12% tax credit on allowable R&D costs |

Claims can only be made for R&D projects that make certain scientific or technological advances. Visit [gov.uk](https://www.gov.uk/corporation-tax-research-and-development-rd-relief) for eligibility details.

### The Patent Box

The Patent Box lets you apply a lower rate of corporation tax (10%) to profits from your patented inventions. To qualify, your company must:

- Be liable for corporation tax
- Make a profit from exploiting patented inventions
- Own or have exclusive rights to the patents
- Have undertaken qualifying development

Visit [gov.uk](https://www.gov.uk/corporation-tax-the-patent-box) for further details.

## Regional Support

### Wales

**Business Wales** offers an innovation support package with specialists to help with R&D, product design and development. Key programmes include:

- **Expertise Wales** --- support and funding for start-ups and small firms developing innovative products. Visit [businesswales.gov.wales](https://businesswales.gov.wales/expertisewales/support-and-funding-businesses)
- **SMARTCymru** --- non-repayable financial assistance for R&D of technically innovative products and processes. Visit [businesswales.gov.wales](https://businesswales.gov.wales/expertisewales/smartcymru)
- **SMARTInnovation** --- support for growing R&D investment including technology acquisition and IP advice. Visit [businesswales.gov.wales](https://businesswales.gov.wales/expertisewales/smartinnovation)
- **SMARTExpertise** --- support for industry-led R&D projects in partnership with research organisations. Visit [businesswales.gov.wales](https://businesswales.gov.wales/expertisewales/smartexpertise)
- **Innovation Vouchers** --- between £2,000 and £25,000 for capital investment in technology or commissioning third-party R&D. Visit [businesswales.gov.wales](https://businesswales.gov.wales/innovation/support-and-funding/funding)

### Scotland

- **SMART: SCOTLAND** --- grants for feasibility studies (up to £100,000, covering up to 70% of costs) and R&D projects (up to £600,000, covering up to 35% of costs). Visit [scottish-enterprise.com](https://www.scottish-enterprise.com/support-for-businesses/funding-and-grants/growing-your-business/smart-scotland-grant)
- **Scottish Enterprise R&D Grant** --- project funding of up to 50% for companies developing new products, processes and services. Visit [scottish-enterprise.com](https://www.scottish-enterprise.com/support-for-businesses/funding-and-grants/growing-your-business/research-and-development-grant)
- **Innovate Your Business** --- one-to-one support and grants of up to £15,000 from Highlands and Islands Enterprise. Visit [hie.co.uk](https://www.hie.co.uk/business-support/innovation-r-d/iyb.html)
- **CAN Do Innovation Challenge Fund** --- up to 100% project funding for innovations solving social challenges. Visit [hie.co.uk](https://www.hie.co.uk/business-support/innovation-r-d/can-do-innovation-fund.html)

### Northern Ireland

- **Invest NI** --- financial support for R&D including linking to universities, developing products/processes and commissioning research. Visit [investni.com](https://www.investni.com/support-for-business/products-and-services/first-time-research-and-development.html)
- **Proof of Concept techstart NI** --- grants of up to £25,000 to fully develop and test your ideas. Visit [investni.com](https://www.investni.com/support-for-business/proof-of-concept-techstart.html)
- **Innovation Vouchers** --- up to £5,000 towards external expert services for idea development, knowledge acquisition or product testing. Visit [investni.com](https://www.investni.com/support-for-business/innovation-vouchers.html)
