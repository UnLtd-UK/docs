## Why Role Descriptions Matter

Clear role descriptions help prospective board members understand what is expected of them and help your organisation recruit the right people. They also provide a basis for evaluating board member performance.

The templates below can be adapted to suit your organisation's size, sector and governance structure.

## Non-Executive Director (NED)

### Role Purpose

To contribute to the strategic direction and governance of the organisation by providing independent oversight, constructive challenge and expert advice.

### Key Responsibilities

- Attend and actively participate in board meetings (typically 4--6 per year)
- Read board papers in advance and come prepared to contribute
- Contribute to the development and monitoring of the organisation's strategy
- Scrutinise the performance of management in meeting agreed goals and objectives
- Ensure the organisation's financial affairs are conducted properly
- Represent the organisation positively when appropriate
- Participate in board committees as required

### Person Specification

- Experience in a relevant professional field (specify areas of expertise needed)
- Understanding of or willingness to learn about the organisation's sector
- Good communication and interpersonal skills
- Ability to think strategically and analyse complex issues
- Commitment to the organisation's values and mission
- Availability to dedicate approximately 1--2 days per month

### Terms

- Initial term of [3] years, renewable for one further term
- Unremunerated (reasonable expenses reimbursed) *or* stipend of [amount] per annum
- Expected to attend [number] board meetings per year plus an annual away day

## Chair of the Board

### Role Purpose

To lead the board in setting strategic direction, ensure effective governance and act as the primary link between the board and the CEO/Managing Director.

### Additional Responsibilities (Beyond Those of a NED)

- Chair board meetings effectively, ensuring all directors can contribute
- Set the board agenda in consultation with the CEO and company secretary
- Ensure the board receives accurate, timely and clear information
- Lead the annual board evaluation process
- Conduct the CEO's performance review (with input from other directors)
- Act as the organisation's ambassador at a senior level
- Mediate any conflicts within the board or between the board and management
- Lead board recruitment and succession planning

### Person Specification (in Addition to NED Requirements)

- Significant board experience, ideally including previous chairing experience
- Strong leadership and facilitation skills
- Ability to build consensus while ensuring robust challenge
- Good understanding of governance best practice
- Availability to dedicate approximately 2--4 days per month

## Treasurer / Finance Director

### Role Purpose

To provide financial oversight and ensure the organisation's financial affairs are conducted with integrity, transparency and in compliance with legal requirements.

### Additional Responsibilities

- Oversee the preparation and presentation of financial reports to the board
- Chair the finance or audit committee (if applicable)
- Ensure the organisation has appropriate financial controls in place
- Oversee the annual audit or independent examination process
- Advise the board on financial strategy, budgets and financial risk
- Ensure the organisation meets its financial reporting obligations

### Person Specification

- Professional financial qualification (e.g. ACA, ACCA, CIMA) or substantial financial experience
- Experience of financial management and reporting
- Understanding of charity or social enterprise finance (or willingness to learn)
- Ability to present financial information clearly to non-financial colleagues

## Charity Trustee

### Role Purpose

To ensure the charity is carrying out its purposes for the public benefit, is well run and delivers the outcomes it was set up to achieve.

### Key Responsibilities

- Ensure the charity complies with charity law and the requirements of the Charity Commission (or OSCR in Scotland)
- Act in the charity's best interests at all times
- Manage the charity's resources responsibly
- Act with reasonable care and skill
- Ensure the charity is accountable to its stakeholders
- Contribute actively to the board's role in giving strategic direction

### Eligibility

You cannot serve as a charity trustee if you:

- Have an unspent conviction for an offence involving dishonesty or deception
- Are an undischarged bankrupt or have a current composition or arrangement with creditors
- Have been removed as a trustee by the Charity Commission or the courts
- Are disqualified from acting as a company director

## Adapting These Templates

When using these templates for your organisation:

- Replace placeholder text (shown in [brackets]) with your specific details
- Add any sector-specific requirements relevant to your work
- Include information about your organisation's mission, size and current priorities
- Specify the skills and experience that are most needed based on your board skills audit
- Ensure terms and conditions align with your Articles of Association
