Background to the Toolkit
Despite diverse-led and equality-focused SPOs being best placed to meet the
needs of disadvantaged communities, underrepresented entrepreneurs are
significantly under-funded and there is a lack of focus on organisations tackling
inequality.
Social purpose organisations (“SPOs”) can sustainably tackle inequality. They
create more jobs than the private sector (at a rate of 3 vs 0.66 per £100,000
turnover) and are more likely to work in communities facing higher levels of
disadvantage; e.g. Disabled people, who have an employment gap of 28%.

Aim of the Toolkit
A society where social purpose organisations pioneer inclusive, diverse and
equitable practice.
By identifying barriers to inclusion, we will provide open-source access to tools,
knowledge and resources that unlock the tangible changes needed to dismantle
systemic inequality.

What is the Toolkit?
The Toolkit provides social purpose organisations with a framework to aid their
understanding of how DEI fits into their existing organisational structure,
operations and culture across multiple areas including but not limited to: gender,
ethnicity, race, disability, sexual orientation, socio-economic disadvantage, and
lived experience.

### 2. Context Setting

### What are the aims of the Toolkit for the fund

Aims for the fund

To understand EDI embeddedness and potential in an organisation by analysing appropriate commitments, policies, processes and practices currently in place.

To summarise how the organisation creates an equal, diverse and inclusive environment for staff, customers and stakeholders for the Investment Committee and Impact Advisory Committee.

To create an EDI action plan for each organisation that receives GIF investment based on key areas for improvement identified in the EDI Toolkit.

Wider aim: A society where social purpose organisations pioneer inclusive, diverse and equitable practice.

### What is the Toolkit?

A framework (in excel) that guides a standardised, in-depth, and structured conversation about EDI. The areas covered have been shown to be key in driving EDI in social purpose organisations according to the EDIT team’s 2021 Literature Review.

Within the excel there are 10 themes (Sections) that were identified in the literature review broken down into smaller Parts and even smaller Level 1, 2 & 3 Statements.

Each ‘Level Statement’ has a drop down to select the progress made by the venture.

### What is the Toolkit?

Through this framework we hope to identify:

Whether organisations are committed, actioning or embedding each statement.

What evidence they have of these actions so far.

What support they need to continue to improve EDI in their organisation.

### 3. Toolkit Deep-dive

### What happens to the outputs?

The facilitator processes the primary output (the Toolkit)

The scorecard summary will automatically be completed by the excel.

For each section, review the evidence recorded in the ‘What we have done’ boxes and evaluate the evidence of these activities shared with you.

Using this review, summarise the highlights; key qualitative (written) information shared by the organisation that will contextualise the scores; and areas where an organisation is early on in their journey.

Finally bullet point the support areas identified during the facilitation to summarise post-investment support needs.

Secondary outputs are created

Investment memorandum write up.

Summary of support needs for post-investment team.

An action plan and audit for the venture whether they receive investment or not.

A first version of EDIT data to be reviewed annually.

### Why are there different Toolkit versions?

There are 3 versions of the EDI Toolkit for use with social enterprises. These were developed based on the first 11 Toolkit completions due to the relevance of certain statements to organisations of different sizes.

### What is the (typical) facilitator user experience?

Read Pre-Due Diligence papers about the organisation and research any further information needed to understand the business model.

Run the introductory presentation call the venture team who will be filling in the Toolkit with you.

Prepare a blank EDIT template before the call.

During the Toolkit facilitation (suggested agenda): 2 hours

-Introductions & accessibility needs check

-Aim of the session

-Open the excel and share your screen so the venture staff can see what you are typing

-Work through each section completing the ‘What we have done box’ with the organisation

-Upon the completion of the Toolkit, spell check and send the excel to the venture for their records and feedback/ further notes as needed.

Finally, analyse the qualitative and quantitative output (as previously described) to write the Investment Memorandum section.
