**Spark** is one of two pillars in UnLtd's support framework. It focuses on your personal growth as a leader and the impact you want to achieve. Whether you are building confidence, developing your leadership skills, or learning how to measure and communicate your social impact, Spark gives you the tools to grow alongside your venture.

## Leading Well

Your wellbeing and leadership development matter as much as your venture's growth. These resources help you look after yourself while building the skills to lead effectively.

| Resource | What you will learn |
| --- | --- |
| [Looking After Your Wellbeing](/spark/leading-well/wellbeing) | Strategies for managing stress, maintaining balance, and sustaining yourself as a founder |
| [Connecting with Partners & Peers](/spark/leading-well/connecting-partners-peers) | How to build meaningful relationships with other social entrepreneurs and supporters |
| [Managing People & Organisations](/spark/leading-well/managing-people-organisations) | Practical guidance on leading teams and managing organisational challenges |
| [Strategic Development & Coaching](/spark/leading-well/strategic-development-coaching) | Tools and approaches for thinking strategically about your venture's direction |

## Measuring Social Impact

A structured approach to understanding and communicating the difference your venture makes, broken into four clear stages.

| Stage | What's involved |
| --- | --- |
| [Plan](/spark/measuring-social-impact/plan) | Define what you want to measure and set up your approach |
| [Do](/spark/measuring-social-impact/do) | Collect your impact data using the right methods |
| [Assess](/spark/measuring-social-impact/assess) | Analyse your data and draw meaningful conclusions |
| [Review](/spark/measuring-social-impact/review) | Share your findings and use them to improve |

## How you can access support

Your Support Manager will help you identify which Spark resources are most relevant to you right now. You can also access support through:

- **One-to-one coaching** — at least 4 sessions through your award
- **Workshops** — free sessions on social impact, leadership and more
- **Peer connections** — Connect and Share events with other social entrepreneurs
- **Mentoring** — expert advice from our network of business professionals
