Looking after your wellbeing is essential as a social entrepreneur. The demands of building a social venture can be intense, and it is important to regularly check in with yourself across all areas of your life.

## Wheel of Life

The Wheel of Life is a coaching tool that helps you assess how balanced and fulfilled you feel across different areas of your life. By rating your satisfaction in areas such as health, relationships, career and personal growth, you can identify where to focus your energy.

<Tip>
  Take time regularly to reflect on your wellbeing. Use the Wheel of Life exercise to check in with yourself and identify areas where you may need more support.
</Tip>
