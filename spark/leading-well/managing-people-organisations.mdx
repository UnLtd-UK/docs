These tools help you align your team around shared goals, understand each other's strengths, and establish clear ways of working together.

## Team Alignment

The Team Alignment worksheet helps you and your team get on the same page about your venture's direction. Use it to surface different perspectives on priorities, goals and ways of working, then discuss and align around a shared understanding.

  Download this visual, single-page worksheet designed to be printed and completed as a team exercise.
</Card>

## Team Canvas

The Team Canvas is a strategic framework that helps teams align on their goals, roles, values and rules. It provides a structured template for team discussions, helping you establish strong foundations for how your team works together.

<Card title="Team Canvas" icon="file-pdf" href="/spark/leading-well/assets/team-canvas.pdf">
  Download this visual canvas template designed to be printed and completed collaboratively.
</Card>

## Team Charter

A Team Charter establishes the ground rules for how your team operates. It covers expectations around communication, decision-making, meeting cadence and accountability, creating a shared reference point for team behaviour.

<Card title="Team Charter" icon="file-pdf" href="/spark/leading-well/assets/team-charter.pdf">
  Download this visual charter template designed to be printed and completed as a team.
</Card>

<Tip>
  These worksheets work best when completed as a group exercise. Set aside dedicated time for your team to work through them together — the conversation is as valuable as the completed document.
</Tip>
