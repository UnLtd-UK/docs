## Why your pitch deck matters

Your pitch deck is often the first detailed look an investor gets at your organisation. It needs to be clear, compelling, and structured so that someone unfamiliar with your work can quickly understand what you do, why it matters, and why they should invest.

## The ten sections of a strong pitch deck

### 1. Introduction (Why you)

Tell investors who you are, why you are here, and what motivates you. This is your chance to make a personal connection and establish credibility from the outset.

### 2. Impact

- Who are your beneficiaries?
- What are the unique needs, challenges, or barriers they face?
- How will your programme, service, or product address those needs?

Lead with impact -- social investors want to see that purpose drives your organisation.

### 3. Market size and opportunity

- Identify two or three problems your programme, product, or service will tackle.
- Present the market size and opportunity in measurable numbers.
- Use data to show that the problem is significant and growing.

### 4. Solution

Present a clear and concise solution that investors can follow easily. Avoid jargon. Explain what you do in plain language, and show how your solution connects directly to the problems you have identified.

### 5. Programme, service, or product

Show off the actual product or service your business delivers. Use screenshots, images, or brief demonstrations where possible. Make it tangible.

### 6. Traction (History of trading)

Focus on the growth you have already achieved:

- Revenue trajectory and key milestones.
- Customer or beneficiary numbers.
- Major goals reached and next steps planned.

### 7. Team

Introduce your core team members and your board or advisors. Highlight relevant experience, complementary skills, and why this team is uniquely positioned to deliver.

### 8. Competition and peers

Define your competition and explain why you are different. Use a simple comparison table if it helps investors see your positioning at a glance.

### 9. Financials (Headline)

- Show how your business makes and spends money in service of social impact and business growth.
- Show how investment and your pipeline of customers will grow your income.
- Keep this high-level in the deck -- detailed financials belong in your financial model.

### 10. Investment and use of funds

Tell investors what you need from them and why:

- How much are you raising?
- What will the money be used for?
- What milestones will the investment help you reach?

## Tips for a stronger pitch deck

- **Keep it concise.** Aim for 10 to 15 slides. Investors review many decks, so respect their time.
- **Tell a story.** Your deck should flow logically from problem to solution to opportunity.
- **Use visuals.** Charts, images, and diagrams are more effective than walls of text.
- **Tailor it to your audience.** Different investors have different priorities. Adjust your emphasis accordingly.
- **Practice your delivery.** The deck supports your pitch -- it does not replace it. Know your material cold.
