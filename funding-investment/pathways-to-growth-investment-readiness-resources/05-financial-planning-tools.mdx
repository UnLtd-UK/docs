## Financial Model Template

This spreadsheet provides a detailed financial modelling template designed for social enterprises seeking investment. It covers a five-year projection period with monthly breakdowns.

<a href="/funding-investment/assets/financial-model-example-2024.xlsx">Download the Financial Model Example 2024 (XLSX)</a>

### Workbook Structure

The model contains six sheets:

| Sheet | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Dashboard** | Summary overview (to be customised with your key metrics) |
| **Assumptions** | Key business assumptions across 5 years, including start dates, unit volumes, pricing, and growth rates |
| **Monthly Cashflow** | Month-by-month cash flow projections covering income, expenses, and net position |
| **Monthly Cashflow Worst Case** | Downside scenario analysis with adjusted assumptions |
| **Annual Summary** | Yearly aggregated financial summary |
| **Staffing Example** | Detailed staffing plan with roles, costs, and headcount projections |

### Key Features

- **Five-year projections** starting from April 2025 through March 2030
- **Monthly granularity** for detailed cash flow tracking
- **Worst case scenario** sheet for stress-testing assumptions
- **Staffing model** with detailed role-by-role cost breakdowns
- **Assumptions sheet** that drives the entire model, making it easy to adjust key variables

### How to Use the Financial Model

1. Start with the **Assumptions** sheet to enter your organisation's specific variables
2. Review the **Monthly Cashflow** sheet to see how assumptions translate to monthly projections
3. Check the **Monthly Cashflow Worst Case** sheet to understand downside risks
4. Use the **Annual Summary** for a high-level financial overview
5. Customise the **Staffing Example** sheet to reflect your team structure and growth plans
6. Update the **Dashboard** to present key metrics to investors

## Cash Flow Forecast and Actual Template

This spreadsheet template helps you track monthly cash flow by comparing projected (forecast) figures against actual results across a full year.

<a href="/funding-investment/assets/cash-flow-forecast-and-actual-template.xls">Download the Cash Flow Forecast and Actual Template (XLS)</a>

### Workbook Overview

The workbook contains two sheets:

#### Explanation Sheet

The explanation sheet provides guidelines for using the spreadsheet:

1. Save the file to your computer under a new name before beginning work
2. Enter your organisation's information in the blue-text cells, using rounded numbers to the nearest dollar
3. Use the suggested categories for each entry, with additional "other" spaces for custom items
4. Use the outline function (plus/minus buttons) to expand and contract detail levels
5. Track Monthly Operating Cash Flow Forecast versus Monthly Actual at the bottom of the sheet
6. The template is designed to print on legal size paper

#### Year 1 Cashflow Sheet

A 12-month cash flow tracking grid with the following structure:

| Section | Details |
|---------|---------|
| **Beginning Total Cash** | Total cash including operating, non-operating, and temporarily restricted cash at the beginning of each month |
| **Income** | Six income line items (Income 1 through Income 6) with a Total Income row |
| **Expenses** | Nine expense line items (Expense 1 through Expense 9) with a Total Expenses row |
| **Net Cash from Operations** | Operating cash at the beginning of the month plus receipts minus disbursements |
| **Loan Activity** | Capital and interest loan payments |
| **Ending Cash** | Net Cash from Operations minus loan payment cash out |

Each month has two columns: **Forecast** and **Actual**, allowing you to compare projections against real results. A **Total** column summarises the full year.

## Using These Tools Together

For a comprehensive financial picture when preparing for investment, use both templates together:

1. **Start with the Financial Model** to build your five-year projections and test assumptions. This gives investors the long-range view they need.
2. **Use the Cash Flow Forecast** for detailed month-by-month tracking once you are operational. This helps you demonstrate financial discipline and identify cash flow gaps early.
3. **Update both regularly** as your actuals come in, so you can show investors how your performance compares to projections.
