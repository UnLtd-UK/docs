## Overview

This directory brings together over 80 UK grant funders relevant to social enterprises, charities, CICs, and community organisations. Each entry includes the fund purpose, typical award amounts, eligibility, geography, application timelines, and a link to the funder's website.

Funders are grouped into thematic sections and **ordered by maximum grant size (largest first)** within each section, so you can quickly find opportunities at the right scale for your organisation. Many funders accept rolling applications, but always check the funder's website for the latest deadlines and eligibility criteria before applying.

<Note>
  Funding information can change. Always verify details directly with the funder before preparing an application. This directory was last updated in February 2026.
</Note>

  </Card>

    Many funders have specific criteria around income thresholds, geography, or organisational type. Read guidelines thoroughly before applying.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Track your pipeline" icon="list-check">
    Use the [Grant Funding Resources and Tracking Tools](/funding-investment/grants/01-grant-funding-resources) spreadsheet to manage applications and deadlines.

  <Card title="Tailor every application" icon="pen">
    Avoid generic proposals. Show how your work aligns with the funder's specific priorities and theory of change.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
