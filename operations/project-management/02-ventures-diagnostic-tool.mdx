## What is the Ventures Diagnostic?

The Ventures Diagnostic and Growth Platform is a structured assessment tool designed to measure a venture's current stage of development, plan targeted growth actions, and track progress over a support period. It provides a shared framework between venture managers and the ventures they support.

This tool is designed as a **working document** — share it with the venture after completing the initial diagnostic to ensure mutual understanding and encourage a collaborative relationship. The role of the venture manager is to work *with* ventures as they strive for impact growth, not to dictate work they must undertake.

## How the Tool is Structured

The diagnostic is organised into seven interconnected sheets:

| Sheet | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| **Covering Page** | Instructions for how to use the tool with ventures |
| **Overarching Ambition** | Captures the venture's overall ambition, support objectives, and success criteria |
| **Diagnostic (Start)** | Assesses the venture's stage at the beginning of the support programme |
| **Growth Journey** | Plans actions, timelines, outputs, and resources needed for growth |
| **Diagnostic (End)** | Reassesses the venture's stage at the end of the support programme |
| **Distance Travelled** | Automatically calculates progress between start and end diagnostics |

## The Assessment Framework

The diagnostic assesses ventures across **three pillars** and **eight support areas**:

### Pillar 1: Person / People

| Support Area | What It Covers |
| --- | --- |
| **1. Leadership** | CEO strategic leadership, performance management, resource allocation, sector knowledge |
| **2. Team Building** | Key functional competencies, senior management team, resourcing, team development |
| **3. Governance** | Overall governance structures, board oversight and accountability |

### Pillar 2: Proposition

| Support Area | What It Covers |
| --- | --- |
| **4. Social Impact** | Theory of change, key metrics, data collection, reporting, social lock |
| **5. Sales Planning** | Value proposition, market knowledge, network building, sales funnel, forecasting, marketing |

### Pillar 3: Performance

| Support Area | What It Covers |
| --- | --- |
| **6. Legals and Reporting** | Incorporation, partner agreements, intellectual property, legal compliance |
| **7. Financial Management** | Financial controls, budgeting, cash flow management |
| **8. Operations** | Operational processes, systems, and efficiency |

## Maturity Levels

For each support objective, you assess the venture against four maturity levels:

| Level | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Unproven, no evidence** | Earliest stage — the capability or practice does not yet exist |
| **Early proof, some evidence** | Some initial steps have been taken with early evidence of progress |
| **Proven, well evidenced** | The capability is established with solid supporting evidence |
| **Highly proven, significant evidence** | Mature capability with extensive evidence and track record |

## Using the Diagnostic

### Setting Overarching Ambition

Capture the venture's long-term ambition as a brief summary of their aims and purpose. Then define:

- **Support objectives** — the desired outcomes over the award period (e.g. achieving first trading income, demonstrable social impact, financial sustainability, geographic growth, team growth, trading partnerships)
- **Success criteria** — measurable indicators for each objective (e.g. number of products delivered, case studies, reaching breakeven, employing additional staff, revenue growth by a stated percentage)

### Running the Start Diagnostic

Assess the venture's current stage at the beginning of the support programme:

1. Work through each support area and its individual objectives
2. Enter explanatory text into the relevant maturity level box
3. Summarise why the venture falls within this stage and not another
4. Only enter text into **one stage box per pillar objective**

### Planning the Growth Journey

Based on the start diagnostic, plan actions for each support objective:

- **Action** — what needs to happen to help the venture grow
- **Time** — when this action should be completed
- **Output** — what the result of this work will be
- **Resources** — tools, people inside or outside the organisation that are needed

### Running the End Diagnostic

At the end of the support programme, repeat the assessment using the same framework. The **Distance Travelled** sheet will automatically calculate the change between start and end scores, giving a clear picture of progress made.
