## What is the EDI Toolkit?

The Equality, Diversity and Inclusion (EDI) Toolkit is a practical self-assessment framework developed by Big Issue Invest, Social Value UK, and UnLtd. It helps social purpose organisations (SPOs) identify barriers to inclusion and build a clear pathway for embedding EDI practice into their operations.

The toolkit aims to contribute to a society where social purpose organisations pioneer inclusive, diverse and equitable practice. By identifying barriers to inclusion and building a framework for organisational learning and development, it develops actionable steps to unlock the tangible changes needed to dismantle systemic inequality.

## How the Toolkit is Structured

The toolkit is organised around **10 EDI factors** (sections) identified through a comprehensive EDI literature review:

| Section | Focus Area |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Understanding EDI Concepts |
| 2 | Mission and Policies |
| 3 | Theory of Change |
| 4 | EDI Advocacy |
| 5 | Data Analysis and Evaluation |
| 6 | Recruitment and Retention |
| 7 | Stakeholder Engagement |
| 8 | Accessibility |
| 9 | Safeguarding |
| 10 | EDI Score Outcomes |

Each section is broken down into **parts**, which form the basis for individual criteria points. For each part, there are **three progressive levels** that your organisation can work through as your EDI practice develops:

- **Level 1** — Commitment: your organisation has committed to taking action
- **Level 2** — Implementation: you have begun putting plans into practice
- **Level 3** — Embedding: practices are fully embedded, reviewed, and continuously improved

## How to Use the Toolkit

1. **Read the section introductions** — background information and reference reading are located at the top of each section page
2. **Complete each section** — select the most relevant response from the dropdown options for each part, indicating your organisation's current level
3. **Provide evidence** — in the "What we have done" column, briefly describe the evidence supporting your selection (e.g. policies, emails, website content)
4. **Add notes** — use the optional notes field to track additional information or leave comments for colleagues
5. **Review your scores** — your overall EDI summary is calculated in the final "EDI Score Outcomes" sheet

### Regular Reviews

The toolkit is designed as a living document. By evidencing your actions, your organisation and stakeholders can track progress over time. Suggested review intervals are **annual or biannual**, depending on your organisation's preference. When used during an investment process, an annual review is recommended.

## Key Sections Explained

### Understanding EDI Concepts (Section 1)

This section covers your organisation's formal training on EDI concepts and the efforts you make to increase understanding. It includes:

- **EDI Characteristics** — training that goes beyond the nine protected characteristics in the Equality Act 2010 to include characteristics such as class and neurodivergence
- **Understanding organisational power** — training on what organisational power means and how it operates within your organisation

### Mission and Policies (Section 2)

This covers how your organisation outlines its commitment to EDI in your mission statement. Your mission and policies should be created alongside people with relevant lived experiences and, where possible, made public. This section includes:

- **Social mission** — a clear organisational commitment to improving EDI
- **Individual responsibilities** — ensuring all staff understand their EDI obligations
- **EDI policies** — formal policy documents that resource and embed accountability

### Theory of Change (Section 3)

Developing an EDI-specific theory of change allows your organisation to define how to approach EDI issues through strategic business choices and to understand both intended and possible unintended outcomes.

### EDI Advocacy (Section 4)

This covers the promotion of EDI within your organisation through training, capacity building, and resource allocation for EDI management.

### Data Analysis and Evaluation (Section 5)

This section addresses how your organisation collects, analyses, and uses EDI data to track progress and inform decision-making.
